Cybersecurity Awareness - Introduction

Cybersecurity protects computers, networks, accounts, and personal information from unauthorized access and cyberattacks.

Phishing is a cyberattack in which criminals use fraudulent emails, messages, or websites to trick people into revealing sensitive information.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) adds an additional layer of security by requiring more than one form of verification.

Strong passwords should be unique for each account. A password manager can help users create and store strong passwords.

Ransomware is malicious software that encrypts files or disrupts access to systems, often demanding payment.

Regular software updates, secure backups, and employee cybersecurity awareness training help reduce security risks.
